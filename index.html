About / 02

Blue Kid On The Rock is the recording project of Pierpaolo Abbatiello — genre-bending electronica with dream-pop sensibilities. Hazy, washed-out choral layering beneath a baritone voice that has drawn comparisons to Matt Berninger and Lou Reed; an 80s DNA reinterpreted in a contemporary key.

Trained at the Fonderie Sonore Academy in Rome, and having lived across Ireland and the United States, he has released nine records since 2018 — among them Summer Platitudes, Telemachus and Silver Line. For licensing, press or anything else: hello@bkotr.com.
